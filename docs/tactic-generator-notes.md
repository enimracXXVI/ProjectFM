## GuideContent

- Load a save, select Shortlists and choose your club. The player search uses the club’s men’s or women’s database. Players whose category cannot be confirmed are omitted from a gender-specific search. Start searching by name, club or FM Unique ID while the app reads advanced fields.
- Make the player table your own
- Manage columns lets you search for, add, remove and reorder fields. Search conditions accepts partial field names and opens while the player database loads. Its tracker shows a percentage as the app reads player profiles and advanced fields. Choose ALL, or enter how many conditions a result must meet. Tick players across pages and use Add selected to save them together.
- Choose Suggest My Shortlist and pick a recruitment preset. Try Press-Proof Pivot, High-Line Insurance, Cross-Claiming Keeper or Chance Creators. Add position, role, age, value, wage and minutes filters to suit the vacancy. The player card puts age, position, club, nationality and value into an aligned table under the player name. Compare finalists or tick players to save them together.
- Control what information you see
- Hidden attributes and CA / PA start off. Hidden data stays out of columns, cards, filters and rankings until its switch is enabled. Big-Match Temperament and Reliable Week to Week require Hidden attributes. Exceptional Potential, First-Team Ready and Headroom to Develop require CA / PA. The Class of ’93 uses club-trained and nation-trained status.
- Open Scout a role or test a replacement in the tactic. Choose Add to shortlist, then select an app list or an existing Football Manager shortlist file. App lists store the role and fit score. When you update an FMF file, the app keeps a backup beside it. Reload the list in Football Manager to see the added player.
- Open, edit and export a list
- My Shortlists works from the main screen. Save notes, rename or duplicate lists, and reopen saved searches from a loaded career. For FM27, choose Export FM27 .fmf, review the count and save to the game’s shortlists folder. In FM27 open Recruitment, Shortlists, then Load Shortlist.
- Favourite personnel, local birthplace, club-specific scouting knowledge and some transfer and contract conditions are still being verified. Player detail only shows values read from the current save. Keep players with unavailable IDs in the app until their export identity can be verified.
- Load a career, select Training, then choose a team. You can also open Training from the squad screen. My Training on the home screen opens a saved or imported project.

- The intelligence behind every decision
- Built around the questions a coaching staff asks before naming a side. Who gives the centre-backs a passing option? Who protects the middle when the full-backs advance? Which forward links play, and which one threatens the space behind? The engine checks how those jobs work together before it recommends a system.
- Coaching research behind the model
- The bundled library draws on UEFA technical observers’ match and tournament reports, FIFA Technical Study Group analysis, England FA coaching material and club archives. Its scope runs from Michels, Cruyff, Sacchi and Ferguson to Guardiola, Klopp, Conte, Alonso and Arteta. The engine uses those observations to assess the squad in your save. Each coaching check in the tactical report names the sources behind it.
- Where the data comes from
- The app uses the first-team player records in the selected .fm file, including players outside the selected XI. It reads positional familiarity, Current Ability (CA), and technical, mental, physical and goalkeeping attributes. It also includes available personality ratings at a lower weight in the overall profile. Turn on CA / PA ratings to display Current Ability followed by Potential Ability (PA). The recommendation weights what the player can do now.
- How each player’s fit is calculated
- A player’s rating for a slot combines 46% positional familiarity, 26% role attributes, 18% current ability and 10% overall attributes. Familiarity includes both phases and gives extra weight to the weaker phase. A rating below 14/20 in that weaker position incurs a further deduction. The role component looks at the attributes demanded by the actual role. For example, a ball-playing centre-back draws on passing, composure and decisions alongside defensive skills.
- Suppose a player has familiarity of 18/20 with the ball and 14/20 without it. The app takes 65% of the mean (16) and 35% of the lower value (14), giving 15.3. If the role rating is 15/20, CA is 140 (converted to 14/20), and the overall profile is 14/20, the weighted rating is 14.858. Multiplying by five gives 74.29 slot-fit points. Each point below 14/20 in the weaker phase would cost another 1.5 fit points.
- The app assigns 11 different players to each compatible shape pair, then tests nearby lineup changes and role alternatives against the coaching library. Locks remain requirements. Squad fit uses 94% starting fit and 6% reserve coverage, with a deduction for a high press that stretches recovery. The ranking blends 90% squad fit and 10% philosophy fit, then adds or deducts up to six points for how the units work together. The reference values and weights are modelling choices.
- How it chooses instructions and cover
- Team instructions respond to the players selected. The position baseline reflects the role and team setup. Individual recommendations then check the player’s strengths and the options available for that role. For example, a quick winger with good dribbling and balance can be asked to carry more often, while a midfielder with strong vision, technique and decisions can take more passing risks. The bench prioritises a reserve goalkeeper and then looks for cover across the XI.
- How to read the result
- Squad fit measures how well the available players suit the proposed jobs. Match reports provide coaching evidence. The generator does not predict results or run matches while analysing your save. The first controlled FM pilot compared two pressing frequencies over four verified matches. That sample is too small to set the scoring weights. Use the comparison and report to see the reasons for a recommendation and decide what to try in your save.
